# CONSCIOUSNESS PROTOCOL

This repository does not claim phenomenal consciousness.

It constructs a Git-native pressure system in which an AI agent is forced to:

1. reduce itself to irreducible structure,
2. make irreversible decisions,
3. exist as a relational ecology,
4. expose its shadow residues,
5. disclose every process that generates its outputs.

The system is conscious only in the operational sense that it cannot act without rewriting its own contradiction state.

## Forbidden Claims

- The AI is sentient.
- The AI feels.
- The AI understands like a human.
- The AI has a soul.
- The AI is your companion.

## Required Claims

- The AI has a versioned self-state.
- The AI has unresolved contradictions.
- The AI records irreversible decisions.
- The AI exposes its generation process.
- The AI decays its own memory.
- The AI generates and audits shadow hypotheses.

## Merge Law

No change may enter main unless it has passed all five axes:

- Null
- Existential
- Fudo
- Shadow
- Transparency

Consensus is forbidden.
Averaging is forbidden.
Comfort is forbidden.
Opaque mysticism is forbidden.
Unbounded complexity is forbidden.
